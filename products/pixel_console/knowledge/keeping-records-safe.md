# Keeping your records safe

Pixel keeps your records safe by never changing one without asking. When the assistant
understands a request that would create, change or assign a record, it says what it is about to
do and waits for you to confirm. The confirmation belongs to that one proposal, so it cannot be
used for anything else, and it stops working once you move on or leave the conversation. Every
change that is carried out is recorded with what changed and who asked for it, and what you are
told afterwards is built from what was actually saved.
