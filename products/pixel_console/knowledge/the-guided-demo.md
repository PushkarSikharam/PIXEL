# The guided demo

The guided demo is a finished product already running on Pixel, so it can be explored without
adding anything. It keeps issues, projects, cycles and teams, and the assistant in it will open
its screens, answer questions about its records, and create and assign work when asked. It is a
product inside Pixel exactly like one you add yourself, which is why it is worth looking at first:
what it does is what your own product will be able to do.
