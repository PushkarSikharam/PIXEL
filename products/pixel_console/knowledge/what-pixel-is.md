# What Pixel is

Pixel is a platform for running products through conversation. A product is described once, in a
definition that says what it keeps, what its screens are called, and what can be done in it. Pixel
reads that definition and gives the product a working assistant, screens and records, without
anyone writing code for it. One Pixel account can run several products at once, and each product
keeps its own records, its own people and its own vocabulary. Nothing crosses from one product
into another.
