# People and roles

People in Pixel belong to an organization, and each person has a role in it. An organization admin
can add products, invite people and change settings. A team admin can do the same for their own
team. Everyone else can use the products they have been given access to. Records can be assigned
to people by name, and who may see a record is decided by the product definition rather than by
the person asking, so nobody can widen what they see by phrasing a question differently.
