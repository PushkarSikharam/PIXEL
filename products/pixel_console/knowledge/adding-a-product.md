# Adding a product

To add a product, describe it: its name, the kinds of record it keeps, and the fields those
records have. Pixel writes the definition from that description and shows it to you before
anything runs on it. When you accept it, the product is added to your organization at version one,
and from then on it has screens, records and an assistant of its own. A product belongs to the
organization that added it and is private to it. Adding a second or a third product changes
nothing about the first.
